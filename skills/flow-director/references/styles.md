# Style library

Starting points for the style bible. Pick one, adjust it to the brief, then paste the same words into every prompt
of the piece. These are not Flow presets; they are wording that holds a look steady across many shots. A style
learned from a user's reference image is described in words the same way; don't copy its people or composition.

Lock one style and one palette per piece. Mixing (a photoreal face on a painted background) looks like an error
unless it is the idea.

## Photographic and film

- **Clean commercial:** bright soft studio light, true colors, crisp detail, shallow depth of field, premium and
  minimal.
- **Natural lifestyle:** window light, warm neutrals, gentle film grain, candid framing, 35-50mm.
- **UGC phone look:** handheld vertical phone video, slightly imperfect framing, natural indoor light, casual and
  authentic, no cinematic grade.
- **Cinematic drama:** anamorphic 2.39-feel framing within 16:9, teal-and-orange grade, motivated practical lights,
  haze, shallow depth of field.
- **16mm documentary:** shot on 16mm film, visible grain, muted colors, handheld, available light.
- **1980s VHS:** soft focus, color bleed, scanlines, slightly washed reds, timestamp-free.
- **Film noir:** black and white, hard low-key light, venetian-blind shadows, wet streets, high contrast.
- **Golden-hour epic:** low warm sun, long shadows, lens flare, sweeping wide shots.
- **Neon night:** wet streets, magenta and cyan neon reflections, light rain, deep shadows.
- **Luxury product:** dark glossy surfaces, single hard rim light, deep blacks, gold accents, slow motion.

## Animation and illustration

- **Pixar-style 3D:** soft rounded characters, expressive eyes, subsurface skin, warm bounce light, rich color.
- **Anime cel:** clean line art, flat cel shading, painted skies, saturated colors, 2D anime film look.
- **Claymation:** matte clay figures with fingerprint texture, miniature sets, slightly jerky stop-motion movement.
- **Storybook:** hand-painted 2D illustration, muted jewel tones, warm light against deep atmospheric shadow,
  expressive characters, detailed environments.
- **Watercolor:** soft watercolor washes, paper texture, bleeding edges, light pastel palette.
- **Flat vector / explainer:** flat shapes, simple geometric characters, limited brand palette, clean motion.
- **Paper cut-out:** layered colored paper, visible edges and soft drop shadows, stop-motion feel.
- **Comic book:** bold ink outlines, halftone dots, strong primaries, dynamic angles.
- **Low-poly 3D:** faceted geometry, soft gradients, pastel lighting, toy-like.

## Explainer and documentary looks

- **Editorial collage:** warm cream paper with fiber grain, black-and-white halftone cut-outs, rough torn edges,
  one accent color, unlabeled abstract diagrams and maps, soft paper shadows, staggered hand-made motion.
- **Paper diorama:** miniature layered cardboard and aged-paper sets, torn edges, light halftone texture, one accent
  color, warm directional light, shallow depth of field, stop-motion movement.
- **Mannequin / clay history:** clean matte clay or mannequin figures, simple period-correct sets, even neutral light,
  smooth weighty movement, consistent silhouettes per figure.
- **Archival:** sepia or faded color photographs, slow Ken Burns push, film scratches.
- **Whiteboard:** hand-drawn black marker on white, a hand drawing the diagram stroke by stroke.

## Children's looks

Age-appropriate 3D, flat 2D, hand-drawn or vector. Characters get one or two big recognizable features (a red cap,
round glasses) and visible reactions. Bright friendly palette, soft light, simple uncluttered backgrounds, the
action named by the narration clearly shown.
